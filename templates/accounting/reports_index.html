Accounting Reports

{% include "accounting/_nav.html" %}

All reports are on a cash basis: income counts when money is received, expenses when they're paid. Money clients still owe is on the Outstanding Payments report.

Income Statement (Profit & Loss)
Income, cost of sales, gross profit, expenses and net profit for a period.
Balance Sheet
Assets, liabilities and equity on a date.
Trial Balance
Every account's debit or credit balance on a date; the two columns must match.
Chart of Accounts
Every account with its balance, grouped by type. Open one for its ledger.
