Close the books

Once a period is closed (for example after the monthly figures or the year-end accounts are done), nothing dated in it can be added, edited or deleted: journals, payments, income or expenses. Mistakes found later are corrected with a new entry in an open period, such as a reversal, so the closed figures never change behind anyone's back.
